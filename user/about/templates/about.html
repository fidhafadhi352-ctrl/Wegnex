OUR HERITAGE & VISION

A LEGACY FORGED IN PASSION & PRECISION

WEGNEX was born out of a profound admiration for the art of mechanical watchmaking. We set out to create luxury timepieces that transcend passing trends, delivering chronometric perfection and imposing architectural presence directly into the hands of connoisseurs.

"A watch does not merely mark the passing seconds; it reflects the wearer's discipline, ambition, and appreciation for extraordinary engineering." — WEGNEX Master Horology Atelier

From hand-finished bevels to anti-reflective sapphire crystals and high-beat automatic movements, every component is scrutinized under 10x magnification. We build heirlooms — instruments destined to be passed down through generations.

UNCOMPROMISING STANDARDS

THE FOUR PILLARS OF WEGNEX HOROLOGY

Every timepiece is an orchestration of metallurgical science, micro-mechanics, and artistic finishing.

Sapphire Crystal

Virtually scratch-proof synthetic sapphire crystals with five-layer interior anti-reflective coating for crystal-clear legibility under any light.

High-Beat Calibers

Calibrated mechanical and automatic movements regulated across multiple positions for chronometer-grade accuracy and buttery-smooth second sweeps.

316L Stainless Steel

Forged from surgical marine-grade 316L stainless steel, combining mirror-polished chamfers with hand-satin brushed flanks for stunning contrast.

Pressure Tested

Engineered with screw-down crowns and dual O-ring gaskets to withstand depth pressures up to 100M-200M, ready for boardroom or open ocean.

THE ATELIER JOURNEY

FROM BLUEPRINT TO WRIST

An exacting process that takes hundreds of hours of precision design and hand-assembly.

Step 01

Architectural Concept

Detailed ergonomic blueprints and 3D modeling to balance dial proportion, bezel geometry, and wrist curvature.

Step 02

Precision Milling

High-precision CNC cutting of case components with micrometer tolerances, followed by multi-stage hand deburring.

Step 03

Master Assembly

Carefully seated gear trains, balance springs, and jewel bearings assembled in ISO-certified cleanroom environments.

Step 04

200-Hour Testing

Each watch undergoes rigorous shock resistance, thermal stability, water pressure, and chronometric variance testing.
